# NESTLE--Sustainable-Sourcing
Nestlé, the world’s largest food and beverage company, operates in 190+ countries with a diverse brand portfolio. Its strengths lie in innovation, R&D, digital transformation, and local market adaptation. Opportunities include AI integration, sustainable sourcing, plant-based products, circular economy initiatives, and e-commerce growth. Challenges involve supply chain complexity, rising raw material costs, sustainability targets, regulatory compliance, and reputational risks like child labor issues. Recommendations focus on strengthening sustainable sourcing, optimizing supply chains, reducing packaging waste, enhancing workforce training, and partnering with startups for innovation. A proactive, tech-enabled, and sustainability-driven strategy is positioned as key to long-term growth.
